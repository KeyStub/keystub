Rules

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the size of the key's ring hole.
  • Navy logo on light backgrounds; white + yellow logo on navy, dark colours or photos.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or recolour the logo outside these versions.
  • Headlines in Archivo ExtraBold; everything else in IBM Plex Sans. No other fonts.
