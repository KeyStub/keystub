Brand naming · rounds 1 & 2

Finding a name for the car-cost tracker

Ten names that passed three tests: they say what the product does, they're easy to draw, and the key domains were unregistered when checked on October 1, 2026. Round 1 is first, then six new ones from round 2, with a side-by-side comparison to help narrow them down.

What the brand stands for

Promise

The honest number behind your car: what it really costs per month and per kilometre, including the things people forget.

Who it's for

Everyday drivers on a budget, families with two cars, and anyone deciding whether to keep fixing the old one or replace it.

Personality

Straight-talking, practical and a little warm. Like a friend who's good with money and knows cars, not a bank and not a gearhead forum.

Proof points

Total cost of ownership, keep-vs-replace, 10-second fill-ups, your data exportable anytime, built in Canada.

Before you commit

  1. Say each name out loud to three people and ask them to spell it back. The one nobody misspells wins ties.
  2. Search the Canadian trademarks database (CIPO) and the US one (USPTO) for your favourite. I found no app conflicts for the top three, but that isn't a legal clearance.
  3. Check social handles (Instagram, TikTok, X) are free.
  4. Buy the .com and .ca together (about $15–25 CAD each per year). Unregistered domains can be taken any day, so don't wait long.
  5. Then I'll set the name across the app and website, turn the logo sketch into a proper logo, and build the colour palette into the design.

"Free" means no registration record existed when I checked the domain registries on October 1, 2026. The logos are quick sketches to judge the idea, not finished artwork.
