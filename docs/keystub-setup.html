02

Make a KeyStub Chrome profile for passwords

10 min · free

Done. All KeyStub work happens in the KeyStub Chrome profile.

You'll need
  • Google Chrome on your computer
  • Your phone, for Google Authenticator
Steps
  1. In Chrome, click your profile picture (top right) → Add → Continue without an account. Name it KeyStub and pick a colour so it's easy to tell apart (Google: multiple Chrome profiles).
  2. Do all KeyStub work in that window from now on. When a sign-up page asks for a password, click the password box and choose Chrome's Use suggested password; Chrome saves it automatically.
  3. In step 04, once admin@keystub.com exists, you'll sign in to this profile and turn on sync. Everything saved so far moves into the KeyStub Google account (Google: sign in to Chrome).
  4. Install Google Authenticator on your phone (App Store or Google Play). It holds the 6-digit codes for two-step login on Porkbun, GitHub, Venn and others (Google Authenticator help).
  5. For things that aren't website logins (your business number, the database connection string, API keys), open Google Password Manager in the KeyStub profile (⋮ → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager), add an entry, and use its Note field.
Why now: you're about to create about 10 business accounts. Unique passwords plus two-step login on each is what keeps the business, and later your customers' data, safe. Keeping them in a KeyStub-only profile keeps them separate from your personal and Vinyl Hut logins.

Done when: a KeyStub Chrome profile exists and Google Authenticator is on your phone.

10

Check the MD of Pincher Creek rules (no business licence)

1 call · $100 if a permit is needed

Done. MD confirmed what (if anything) is needed.

What applies
  • Business licence: not needed. You live in the MD of Pincher Creek No. 9, not the Town, and the MD "does not require or issue Business Licenses" (MD: business licences). Still current as of October 2026.
  • Development permit: ask. The MD's land use bylaw (Bylaw 1349-23) treats any occupation run from a residence as a home occupation, and home occupations aren't on its list of things that skip a permit (Land Use Bylaw 1349-23, Sections 6.80, 15 and 47). Section 15.4 says that when it's unclear, the MD's planning commission decides.
Steps
  1. Find out what you did for The Vinyl Hut. If the MD already gave you a home occupation permit or said none was needed, ask whether KeyStub can be added to it or is covered the same way.
  2. If not, contact the MD's Planning & Development office: 403-627-3130 or info@mdpinchercreek.ab.ca (1037 Herron Avenue) and ask: "I run an online software business from my home office: computer only, no customers visiting, no signs, no employees, no stored materials. Do I need a home occupation development permit?"
  3. If they say yes: fill in the Development Permit Application Form (use: Home occupation, online software business, home office only), attach a simple site plan (a sketch of your lot showing the house and where the office is), and submit it with the fee: $100 for a permitted use. Home occupation is a permitted use in the Agriculture and Grouped Country Residential districts, so $100 is most likely; $150 if it's treated as discretionary. More: MD: how to apply for a development permit.
  4. Ask whether The Vinyl Hut needs the same permit, so one conversation covers both businesses.
  5. Save the permit, or the MD's email saying it isn't needed, in KeyStub Records.

Done when: you have either a home occupation permit or written confirmation from the MD that you don't need one.
